# Multilingual learning icon

The mark avoids letters, flags and a single-language identity. The small speech form becomes two ascending strokes and an arrow: a sentence is transformed step by step. The midnight teal, ivory and amber palette is independent of the current Polish lessons.

`learning-icon-1024.png` is the opaque iOS/Android/web master; `LearningIcon.icns` is the transparent-corner macOS distribution icon. `learning-icon-source.png` and `learning-icon-macos-source.png` preserve the generated originals. The `.iconset` contains the macOS sizes used to build the `.icns`.

Generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool. The selected icon prompt was:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final 1024x1024 application icon for a premium multilingual language-learning tool. Render as truly flat 2D vector-style artwork, exactly three solid colors with hard clean edges, no antialias artifacts visible, no gradients, no lighting, no glow, no texture, no shadows, no transparency. Full-bleed square midnight teal (#17333A) background. Center one compact abstract geometric mark occupying about 58% of the square: a small amber speech dot at lower left, two ivory curved parallel strokes moving diagonally upward and forward as a transformation sequence, ending in one amber forward-turn shape at upper right. The silhouette should suggest a phrase becoming a more precise phrase, and remain legible at 32px. Large even margin for iOS and Android icon masking. Modern Swiss graphic design, impeccably balanced, simple enough to redraw as SVG. No text, no letters, no flags, no globes, no books, no graduation caps, no outlines, no mockup, no border, no watermark.

The macOS variant edited that image with this prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: macOS .icns source, 1024x1024 transparent PNG. Input image is the exact approved app icon. Preserve the speech dot, two ivory flowing strokes, amber arrow, their shapes, positions, scale, colors, and the deep teal background exactly. Change only the outer silhouette: clip the existing full-square teal background to a centered macOS-style squircle / continuous rounded square occupying about 94% of the canvas, with genuinely transparent pixels outside its corners. Do not add shadow, bevel, border, glow, lighting, text, new symbols, or alter the logo itself. Crisp professionally masked edges and transparent corners.
