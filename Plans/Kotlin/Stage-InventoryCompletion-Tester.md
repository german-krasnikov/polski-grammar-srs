## Independent acceptance result

The initial audit found two coverage escapes beyond the planned 33 files: the active shared-native vocabulary screen was absent, and TSX JSX text without quotes was absent. Reviewer follow-up also found multiline string interiors and nested interpolation. The generator now walks production sources and the independent synthetic tests cover all four lexical cases. The current generated inventory has **101 files, 2,365 candidate lines and 4,504 classified tokens**; the checker reports no unknown, stale, duplicate or incomplete parts. Its classification is an editorial claim, so I also sampled the long Russian UI-copy exemptions and checked the five extracted authored groups against the source and pack. I found no further course-authored phrase in that sample. `SpikeHtmlControls.kt` is included by the expanded source walk and must remain explicitly classified as an unreachable prototype rather than silently omitted.
