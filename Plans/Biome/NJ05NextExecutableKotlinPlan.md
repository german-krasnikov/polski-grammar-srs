## Product boundary and verified starting point

Kotlin Multiplatform is the sole target application and data owner. The former `src/textwire/*.ts`, `tests/textwire/*.test.ts` and paired TS/Kotlin product-codec instructions in the historical [TextWireExperimentBlueprint.md](TextWireExperimentBlueprint.md) are superseded. Existing React/TypeScript source and runs may identify historical behavior; an isolated compiler portability comparator may test a general Biome backend, but neither is an NJ05 product codec, an active app oracle, or a required runtime. The next mechanical A/C implementation, if admitted, belongs in **NEW** `kotlin/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/polski/textwire/`, consumed by Kotlin/Swift-host adapters. No new DSL syntax is needed for framing, UTF-8, UTF-16, SHA-256 or storage (P4/P8).

The test-only inputs `tests/fixtures/textwire/{legacy-boundary,numeric-admission}.cases` have exact whole-file seals in `legacy-admission.sha256`: `e3550c49fc7ab34ef4ae08d751a51c9705a47839d801f84a804cb4387914d5d9` and `546ed10dcd69c4e2b4fb95a4194c383304aa0c8151415d1274204e803e692e11`. `positive.cases`, `negative.cases`, `document.cases`, `transaction.cases` have exact hashes in `corpus.sha256`. Twelve A and twelve C positive byte oracles and N16 A/C bytes are frozen; B bytes and N26 numeric bounds are not. **Do not edit any of these six `.cases` files or two hash files in the admission/prototype slice.** A later independent N26 requirements commit may change `negative.cases` and `corpus.sha256` with its own review and reason. The 10 MB accepted progress examples already imply A lengths 10,000,112/10,196,726 and C lengths 40,000,022/39,214,145; these are lower floors from pure arithmetic, not proposed caps or P23 headroom.

The old Kotlin codec is also the live JSON writer today: `ProgressCodec.decode/encodeLegacyV1` in `kotlin/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/polski/progress/ProgressCodec.kt`, `VocabularyCodec.decode/encode` and `VocabularySession.start/importJson/exportJson/commit` in `.../vocabulary/{VocabularyDocument,VocabularySession}.kt`, and `UserPreferencesCodec.decode/encode/peekTargetNative` in `.../preferences/UserPreferencesCodec.kt`. `RawProgressRepository.save/migrateLegacy` in `.../progress/ProgressRepository.kt` writes JSON preview and compares JSON readback; `TrainingStore` can trigger the fresh save. This is the known old baseline, **not** an accepted no-JSON path. Existing tests that call `encodeLegacyV1`, `VocabularyCodec.encode` or `UserPreferencesCodec.encode` can remain historical behavior checks, but must not manufacture new NJ05 legacy oracle bytes or be counted as zero-JSON evidence.

The accepted exception is exact-path, byte-hashed, **input-only old-user JSON** for a one-time, read-only migration decoder and its isolated tests. The new app must never serialize JSON for a save, preview, backup, receipt, export, download, snapshot or test-generated acceptance artifact. E0–E2 must not call any old `encode`, `importJson`, `saveRaw`, `save`, `migrateLegacy`, export or backup path, even in disposable storage. A test harness may inject already sealed fixture bytes under an old key/path in a fresh isolated store, verify their hash, invoke only read-only decode/load/peek/admission, then verify the same bytes remain; it must neither serialize nor overwrite a JSON document. Earlier old-writer/import receipts remain historical evidence at their recorded SHA, and direct write-path behavior on this new SHA is **NOT RUN**. Tool-required configuration JSON is separately inventoried by path and role; it is not permission for app data.
