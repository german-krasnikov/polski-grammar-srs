# No-JSON full port acceptance: Stage 0 ledger

Status: **baseline only; no FP or NJ gate accepted**. Owner requirement and stop conditions: [NoJsonFullPortPlan.md](NoJsonFullPortPlan.md), [ADR-51](../../AI/decisions.md).
