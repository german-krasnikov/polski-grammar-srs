## Fixture and location convention

Use a fresh copy of `courses/lang/en/curriculum.core-data` for **each** mutation. The baseline has `SURFACE curriculum` on line 1, E3 on line 2, E1 on line 3 and E2 on line 4, with a final newline. Each `SKILL` has one complete record on one physical line. Columns below are **one-based UTF-8 source character columns**; all characters before the cited mutation on these lines are ASCII, so byte and character columns coincide. A location is acceptable if it highlights the named token/value or its containing declaration and names the exact bad member or relation. A generic file-only error, an unrelated next line or a diagnostic inferred by string search after parsing is not adequate. For omissions, the containing `SKILL` record begins at column 7. The diagnostic wording/code may differ, but the cause and source relation must be unambiguous. All mutations are isolated unless stated otherwise; apply exact replacements once to the specified line and leave other lines byte-for-byte intact.

The baseline source is the three literal lines printed in the experiment plan. Its key anchors are: line 2 `id` value at column 12 and `prerequisites` at 187; line 3 `id` value at 12, construction value at 46, `focus.to` value at 108, second-level `Person` key at 132, `nounId` first value at 202, `child` at 234, `level` value at 250, and prerequisite value at 269; line 4 `focus` at 54, `fixed` at 66, and `prerequisites` at 106. Assert these anchors against the input fixture before running any candidate. If the candidate source changes, freeze a new version of this sheet before executing it; do not silently move expected positions after seeing diagnostics.
