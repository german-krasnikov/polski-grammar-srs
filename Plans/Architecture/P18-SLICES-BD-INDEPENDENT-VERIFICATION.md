### Reproducible content defects

1. `numerals4Explanation` and `numerals4Reason` in `exerciseCopy.core-data`: `Mam jednego kota.` → `Mam trzy koty.` is correct, but the explanation says `неодушевлённо-неличной группы`. A cat is animate. Expected: describe the non-masculine-personal plural accusative form without classing the animal as inanimate. This misinformation is visible on the card back.
2. The `wordform.nouns`, `sentence.connectives`, `sentence.rephrase`, `case.valency`, and `verb.mood` tips have the source-location errors shown above. `sentence.connectives` additionally ends `nie myl celu z przyczyną ponieważ`, an awkward mixed Russian/Polish fragment rather than a complete learner-facing thought. Expected: accurate, reachable primary-source receipt and a coherent tip. The developer evidence and gap ledger repeat the nonexistent ch. 17 citation.
3. `rephrase5Source` says `Po zakończeniu pracy wrócił do domu.`; `rephrase5Expected` says `Kiedy skończył pracę, wrócił do domu.` The source permits the work to have been finished by someone else, whereas the answer fixes the same actor as the returnee. Expected: an explicit same-actor source or instruction so the transformation preserves participants. The SituationFirst introduction for `sentence.connectives` should cover all six relations; its current cause/contrast wording omits condition, purpose, concession and time.
