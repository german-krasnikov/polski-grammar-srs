## Behavior and ownership

`IosExamSession` owns first committed raw answers, source token IDs, official scorer and shared DSL evaluation, generation guarded automatic focus, and frozen review rows for Grammar, Reading, and Listening. `IosPreferencesSession` exposes the portable default-on preference and strict writes. SwiftUI renders committed controls and neutral return locks, immediate chosen/source/status text, explicit Check for draft inputs, two-second focus in the current group, and submitted report. Its single cancellable task only schedules the bridge command. ON/OFF switching clears practice state while retaining reducer drafts, answers, index, and submitted result. Timed exams retain editable answers and hide the key until part freeze. Same-task Listening feedback/focus retains the player and its playback position.
